Android Setup & Quick Guide

Follow these quick steps on your phone for zero-friction casting.

1

Enable USB Debugging on Android

Open Settings → About Phone, tap Build Number 7 times until Developer Mode is unlocked.
Then go to Settings → System → Developer Options and turn on USB Debugging.

2

Plug In USB Cable & Authorize

Connect your phone to this PC with a USB cable. When the phone prompts with "Allow USB debugging from this computer?", check Always allow and tap Allow.

3

Enable Remote Input Control (Optional)

To control the phone with your mouse and keyboard, open Settings → Accessibility on Android and turn on HSCast Remote Input.

4
